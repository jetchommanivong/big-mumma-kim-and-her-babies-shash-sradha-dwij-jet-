# Interview P3 — Event Photographer

**Research project:** Why We Photograph: Meaning and Motivation Behind the Everyday Photo
**Date:** 21 August 2026

---

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me about the last time you had your photo taken?

**Participant:** Probably the other week.

**Interviewer:** Do you usually get your photo taken at occasions and events, or generally as well?

**Participant:** Not unless my friends are into that kind of thing — aside from phone photography, people just taking pictures of the moment. Not particularly.

**Interviewer:** What's your favourite photo you've taken this year?

**Participant:** I took a picture at a club event. It was themed around a show I hadn't watched, but everyone was really hyped and energetic, which made for some good photos. It's my favourite because it suits the type of photos I like taking — very energetic, flash forward, and everyone looks like they're having fun.

**Interviewer:** If someone scrolled through your camera roll, what impression would they get? What do you think it says about you?

**Participant:** This person doesn't like taking corporate photos. I like flash photography, club events where I don't have to sit in a chair and take photos — I get more creative freedom.

**Interviewer:** When you're out and about, what makes something feel worth photographing?

**Participant:** I don't really take photos when I go outside casually. It's usually professional — event stuff.

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me what you usually do with the photos you take?

**Participant:** It's usually for whatever the client's intended purpose was — either for social media, or as a memory of the event for attendees, so they can look through and go "that's me."

**Interviewer:** Do you have your own social media where you share photos?

**Participant:** No, I have one for my friends where I post whatever, but it's not professional.

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me about a time someone shared a photo of you without asking?

**Participant:** Some of my friends might post pictures of events they host, like birthday parties, and I might be in them.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything else you'd like to talk about — what motivates you to take photographs, or why you chose this as a profession?

**Participant:** It's more of a side hustle. I think it's fun — I usually have headphones on, drowning everything out and taking photos, but even still people like to talk to me for some reason. I think you meet a lot of people that way. At events, sometimes you just stand there awkwardly not knowing what to do, but if you're taking photos, you have a job — it gives you an opportunity to socialise with people, and to be places you might not usually be. It gives you a purpose when you're there.
